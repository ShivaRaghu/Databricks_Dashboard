# Healthcare Claims Dashboard (Databricks)

This dashboard tracks healthcare claims data to provide actionable insights for health centers. The dashboard was built in Databricks and focuses on cost management and utilization trends.

## Key Features & Insights
- **Monthly Claims Trend**: Track both volume and total cost of claims month-over-month.
- **Cost Distribution by Medical Service Category**: Understand where most costs are incurred (e.g., Pharmacy, ER, Office Visits).
- **Top 10 High-Cost Members**: Identify members driving the highest costs.
- **Top 10% Member Cost (%)**: Highlight critical high-cost members for leadership attention.
- **PMPM (Per Member Per Month)**: Measure cost per member and monitor trends.
- **Top 10 High-Cost ICD-10 Codes**: Pinpoint the diagnoses contributing most to costs.

## Purpose
The dashboard enables health centers to:
- Identify cost drivers and high-utilization areas
- Monitor trends in claims and PMPM over time
- Support leadership decisions for care management and cost containment
